# Law Classic

Established authority: oxford navy, parchment and Baskerville, with clear practice areas and partner profiles. Mood: Authoritative, discreet, steady.

## Principles
- Gravitas through restraint: serif type, generous margins, no gimmicks.
- Expertise is specific: practice areas, partners and results.
- Consultation request is clear and confidential.

## Layout
Hero with a classic statement headline and Request a consultation. Practice areas in a 3-column grid, partner profiles, notable cases, credentials, contact with office map.

## Typography
Libre Baskerville headlines 34–56px. Source Sans 3 body 17px/1.7. Small caps labels.

## Color
Parchment base, navy text and primary, antique gold accent for rules and small details only.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Square-ish, navy fill, white label; secondary gold underline link.
- **Cards:** Practice area: thin gold top rule, title, two lines, 'Read more'.
- **Navigation:** Navy top line with phone, white bar with links.
- **Forms:** Classic boxed inputs, confidentiality note under the form.

## Imagery & icons
Formal partner portraits, architecture, documents; thin classic icons.

## Motion
None beyond subtle fades.

## Do
- Partner profiles
- Practice area pages
- Confidentiality note

## Don't
- Bright colours
- Playful illustrations
- Stock gavel images

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Libre Baskerville:400,700, Source Sans 3:400,600.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
