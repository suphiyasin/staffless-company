# Dental Fresh

Bright and minty: fresh aqua, airy white and rounded shapes that make a check-up feel easy. Mood: Fresh, upbeat, reassuring.

## Principles
- Bright and airy: white space and mint tints, never clinical grey.
- Prices and treatments are transparent.
- Online booking in two clicks.

## Layout
Hero with a smiling-patient photo in a rounded frame and a booking card. Treatments as rounded cards with price-from, before/after slider, team, reviews with star ratings, FAQ.

## Typography
Outfit throughout. Headlines 40–60px weight 700. Body 17px/1.65.

## Color
Very light aqua base, white cards, deep aqua primary, coral accent for offers and star ratings.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 12px radius, aqua fill; ghost variant with aqua text.
- **Cards:** White, 24px radius, soft aqua shadow, icon in a mint circle, 'from €' price.
- **Navigation:** White bar, Book online button in aqua.
- **Forms:** Rounded inputs, mint focus ring.

## Imagery & icons
Bright, natural smiles; rounded friendly icons.

## Motion
Soft 200ms lifts; slider transitions.

## Do
- Price-from labels
- Before/after section
- Review stars

## Don't
- Dark colours
- Close-up dental instruments
- Medical jargon

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Outfit:400,600,700.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
