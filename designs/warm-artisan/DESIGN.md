# Warm Artisan

Handmade warmth: terracotta and cream, a characterful serif, textured sections. Mood: Cosy, crafted, inviting.

## Principles
- Food and craft photography do the selling.
- Warm, earthy palette; never cold blues.
- Opening hours, location and booking always one tap away.

## Layout
Full-bleed photo hero with the name in serif, menu in two columns with dotted leaders, gallery grid, map + hours band.

## Typography
Display serif headlines 44–72px; body Karla 17px/1.65; menu prices in tabular figures.

## Color
Cream base, terracotta primary, olive accent, espresso text.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Terracotta fill, 6px radius; 'Book a table' always visible.
- **Cards:** Cream surface, photo top, serif title.
- **Navigation:** Transparent over hero, solid cream on scroll.
- **Forms:** Warm boxed inputs for reservations (date, time, guests).

## Imagery & icons
Warm, close-up food photography with natural light.

## Motion
Slow 300ms fades.

## Do
- Menu with prices
- Hours and map
- Reservation form

## Don't
- Cold greys
- Tech-style gradients
- Tiny photos

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: DM Serif Display:400, Karla:400,500,700.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
