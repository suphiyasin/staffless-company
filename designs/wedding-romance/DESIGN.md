# Wedding Romance

Soft romance: ivory, dusty rose and eucalyptus, delicate serif headings and floral spacing. Mood: Romantic, delicate, timeless.

## Principles
- Delicacy: thin lines, airy spacing, centred compositions.
- The couple's story and the date lead; logistics follow.
- One RSVP action, impossible to miss.

## Layout
Centred hero with names, date and a countdown. Our story timeline, ceremony and reception details in two cards, travel and stay info, gallery, RSVP form.

## Typography
Italiana headings 40–72px with wide tracking. Lora body 17px/1.75; italic for quotes and dates.

## Color
Ivory base, dusty-rose primary for RSVP and links, eucalyptus accent for small floral details.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Square, rose fill or thin rose outline, small caps label.
- **Cards:** Ivory surface, thin border, centred text, small floral divider.
- **Navigation:** Centred links, minimal.
- **Forms:** RSVP: name, attending yes/no, guests, dietary notes, message.

## Imagery & icons
Soft film-like photography, line-drawn botanicals.

## Motion
Slow fades, countdown ticking softly.

## Do
- Countdown
- Story timeline
- RSVP form

## Don't
- Bold sans-serif
- Strong saturated colours
- Dense blocks of text

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Italiana:400, Lora:400,600.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
