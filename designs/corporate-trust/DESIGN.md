# Corporate Trust

Steady and credible: deep blue, structured grids, clear proof points. Mood: Reliable, serious, clear.

## Principles
- Lead with outcomes and numbers.
- Structure over style: clear sections, consistent grid.
- Trust signals early: certifications, clients, testimonials.

## Layout
Split hero (text left, image right), stats row, services grid, case studies, testimonial, contact band.

## Typography
Headlines 36–52px semibold. Body 16px/1.65.

## Color
Navy text, blue primary, teal accent for positive figures, light blue-grey surfaces.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 6px radius, solid blue primary, outlined secondary.
- **Cards:** Light surface, subtle shadow, icon top-left.
- **Navigation:** White bar, blue active underline, phone number visible.
- **Forms:** Boxed inputs, clear labels and help text.

## Imagery & icons
Real team and office photography, simple outline icons.

## Motion
Minimal.

## Do
- Numbers and proof
- Clear CTAs
- Consistent spacing

## Don't
- Playful fonts
- Neon colours
- Vague claims

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: IBM Plex Sans:400,500,600,700.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
