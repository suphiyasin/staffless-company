# Fitness Gym

Training-floor intensity: graphite, electric lime, tall condensed headlines and class timetables. Mood: Driven, loud, motivating.

## Principles
- Action first: real training photography, high contrast, no softness.
- The timetable and the free trial are always reachable.
- Numbers sell: members, classes per week, results.

## Layout
Full-bleed hero with a training photo, a huge two-line headline and Start free trial. Stats strip, class grid, weekly timetable table, coach cards, membership pricing.

## Typography
Anton uppercase headlines 56–112px. Barlow body 17px/1.6; labels in Barlow 600 uppercase.

## Color
Graphite base, lime primary for buttons and highlights, orange accent only for 'full' or 'hot' class tags.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 4px radius, lime fill with graphite text, uppercase.
- **Cards:** Dark surface, photo with a dark gradient, class name in Anton, duration and level chips.
- **Navigation:** Black bar, logo left, Join button in lime.
- **Forms:** Dark inputs, lime focus border.

## Imagery & icons
Gritty, high-contrast training shots; bold filled pictograms.

## Motion
Fast 150ms; counters that tick up once.

## Do
- Weekly timetable
- Big stats
- Free-trial CTA

## Don't
- Pastels
- Thin fonts
- Stock smiling-model photos

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Anton:400, Barlow:400,600,700.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
