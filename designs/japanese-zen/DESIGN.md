# Japanese Zen

Wabi-sabi calm: rice-paper white, sumi ink, a single vermilion seal and asymmetric balance. Mood: Serene, crafted, intentional.

## Principles
- Ma (negative space) is the main material.
- Asymmetric balance: one strong element offset by emptiness.
- One vermilion seal-like accent per page.

## Layout
Hero with a single object photo placed off-centre and a short vertical-feeling headline. Product or story sections alternating sides with wide gutters, a quiet gallery, contact.

## Typography
Noto Serif JP headings 32–56px with open tracking. Noto Sans JP body 16px/1.9.

## Color
Rice-paper base, sumi ink text, vermilion primary used sparingly, moss green accent for small details.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Square, vermilion fill or ink outline, small label.
- **Cards:** No shadows; image and text separated by space and a hairline rule.
- **Navigation:** Sparse text links, logo as a small seal.
- **Forms:** Underlined inputs, generous spacing.

## Imagery & icons
Natural materials, ceramics, wood, shadows; brush-stroke details, very few icons.

## Motion
Slow 500ms fades only.

## Do
- Asymmetry
- Wide margins
- Natural textures

## Don't
- Clutter
- Bright multi-colour palettes
- Cliché cherry-blossom stock

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Noto Serif JP:400,600, Noto Sans JP:400,500.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
