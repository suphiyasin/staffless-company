# Fintech Modern

Confident money app: deep indigo, mint highlights, phone mockups and clean data visuals. Mood: Secure, smart, modern.

## Principles
- Show the app: phone mockups with real-looking numbers.
- Security is part of the message: encryption, licences, protection badges.
- Numbers are precise: fees, rates and limits shown plainly.

## Layout
Split hero with headline, app store buttons and a tilted phone mockup. Feature rows alternating image/text, fee comparison table, security section, testimonials, download CTA.

## Typography
Sora. Headlines 40–64px weight 700, tight tracking. Body 17px/1.6. Figures in tabular numerals.

## Color
Cool off-white base, deep indigo primary, mint accent for positive numbers and success states; red only for negative amounts.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 12px radius, indigo fill; app store badges in black.
- **Cards:** White, 22px radius, soft indigo shadow, icon in an indigo-tinted square.
- **Navigation:** Clean white, Log in text link and Open account button.
- **Forms:** Large inputs with currency prefixes, inline validation.

## Imagery & icons
App UI mockups, simple charts, geometric line icons.

## Motion
Numbers count up once; 200ms transitions.

## Do
- Fee tables
- Security badges
- Phone mockups

## Don't
- Stock handshake photos
- Vague claims
- Gaudy gold

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Sora:400,600,700.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
