# Soft Pastel

Gentle and welcoming: pastel blush and mint, big rounded shapes, soft shadows. Mood: Kind, calm, approachable.

## Principles
- Round everything generously.
- Soft colour fields instead of lines.
- Warm, encouraging microcopy.

## Layout
Hero with blob shapes behind an illustration, centred sections, cards in a relaxed 3-column grid.

## Typography
Rounded sans. Headlines 40–60px. Body 17px/1.7.

## Color
Blush primary, mint accent, plum text. Tinted section backgrounds (blush 8%, mint 10%).

## Components
- **Buttons:** Fully rounded pills, blush fill, soft shadow.
- **Cards:** White, 28px radius, soft shadow, pastel icon circle.
- **Navigation:** Floating rounded bar.
- **Forms:** Rounded inputs with tinted background.

## Imagery & icons
Soft illustrations, gentle photography, rounded icons.

## Motion
Gentle 250ms easing, slight scale on hover.

## Do
- Blob shapes
- Pastel section tints
- Friendly emoji-free copy

## Don't
- Sharp corners
- Pure black
- Aggressive red

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Nunito:400,600,800.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
