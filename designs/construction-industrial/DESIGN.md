# Construction Industrial

Built to last: concrete grey, safety yellow, condensed headlines and project numbers. Mood: Solid, capable, no-nonsense.

## Principles
- Proof through projects: photos, sizes, dates and locations.
- Industrial clarity: flat colour blocks, hard edges, strong grid.
- Safety yellow marks the important things only.

## Layout
Full-bleed site photo hero with a yellow stripe and big condensed headline. Services grid, key numbers band (years, projects, m² built), project gallery with filters, certifications, quote request.

## Typography
Bebas Neue uppercase headlines 48–110px. Roboto body 17px/1.6. Numbers in Bebas Neue.

## Color
Concrete grey base, black primary buttons, safety-yellow accent for stripes, highlights and key numbers backgrounds.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Square, black fill with white label; yellow used for one hero CTA with black text.
- **Cards:** Project: photo, location, year, size; flat, no shadow, thin border.
- **Navigation:** White bar, phone number and Get a quote button.
- **Forms:** Quote form with project type, size, location, timeline.

## Imagery & icons
Real job-site and finished-project photography; bold filled industrial icons.

## Motion
Minimal; counters tick once.

## Do
- Project numbers
- Certifications
- Hazard-stripe accents

## Don't
- Pastels
- Rounded bubbly shapes
- Vague claims without projects

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Bebas Neue:400, Roboto:400,500,700.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
