# Hotel Resort

Sunset at the resort: warm sand, deep teal water and graceful serif type around immersive photography. Mood: Relaxed, luxurious, inviting.

## Principles
- Sell the feeling: full-bleed photography and short evocative copy.
- Availability check is always on screen (sticky booking bar).
- Rooms, dining and experiences each get a calm, image-led section.

## Layout
Full-screen video or photo hero with a sticky booking bar (dates, guests, Check availability). Rooms carousel, experiences in an asymmetric image grid, dining, location map, offers.

## Typography
Gilda Display headlines 40–76px. Lato body 16px/1.75; small uppercase labels with 0.15em tracking.

## Color
Warm sand base, deep teal primary, sunset copper accent for offers and highlights.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Almost square, teal fill, uppercase small label.
- **Cards:** Room cards: large photo, name, size and guests, 'from' price per night.
- **Navigation:** Transparent over hero, turns sand on scroll; Book now on the right.
- **Forms:** Booking bar with date pickers, guest counter and one button.

## Imagery & icons
Golden-hour landscape, pool and room photos; minimal thin icons for amenities.

## Motion
Slow 500ms fades, gentle Ken Burns on the hero.

## Do
- Sticky booking bar
- Room size and guest counts
- Amenity icons

## Don't
- Busy patterns
- Bold sans headlines
- Small images

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Gilda Display:400, Lato:400,700.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
