# Retro 80s

Synth sunset: midnight blue, sunset gradients, chrome-like headlines and a perspective grid horizon. Mood: Nostalgic, flashy, fun.

## Principles
- The sunset gradient (pink → orange) and a grid horizon define the look.
- Headlines feel like chrome or neon signs.
- Keep content readable: effects on headlines only.

## Layout
Hero with a sunset circle, perspective grid floor and a glowing headline. Event or release cards, a lineup or tracklist, gallery, tickets band.

## Typography
Righteous uppercase headlines 44–96px with wide tracking. Chakra Petch body 16px/1.6.

## Color
Midnight blue base, hot pink primary, sunset orange accent; gradients only between these two.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 6px radius, pink fill with dark text and glow; secondary pink outline.
- **Cards:** Indigo surface, thin pink border, glow on hover.
- **Navigation:** Dark, neon logo, Tickets button.
- **Forms:** Dark inputs, pink glow focus.

## Imagery & icons
Synthwave illustrations, palm silhouettes, VHS textures, neon line icons.

## Motion
Slow grid scroll and glow pulse (off for reduced motion).

## Do
- Grid horizon
- Sunset gradient
- Glow on headlines

## Don't
- Glow on body text
- Flat corporate blue
- Thin serif fonts

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Righteous:400, Chakra Petch:400,600.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
