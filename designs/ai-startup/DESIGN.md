# AI Startup

Bright AI launch page: white space, a soft aurora gradient, a prompt-box hero and crisp product shots. Mood: Optimistic, clever, effortless.

## Principles
- The hero demonstrates the product: a prompt box or a live example output.
- One soft aurora gradient (violet → pink) behind the hero only.
- Explain the value in one sentence, then show three concrete use cases.

## Layout
Centred hero with headline, a fake prompt input with example chips, product screenshot below. Use-case cards, how it works in three steps, integrations logo grid, pricing, FAQ.

## Typography
Figtree. Headlines 48–76px weight 800, tight tracking. Body 18px/1.6 in muted grey.

## Color
White base, lavender surfaces, violet primary, pink accent for gradient ends and 'new' tags.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 12px radius, violet fill; secondary white with border.
- **Cards:** Lavender surface, 20px radius, icon, title, short text, example output snippet.
- **Navigation:** White, sticky, Sign in link and Get started button.
- **Forms:** Prompt-style input with a send button inside.

## Imagery & icons
Product UI screenshots, soft gradient blobs, simple line icons.

## Motion
Typing animation in the hero prompt; 200ms fades.

## Do
- Prompt-box hero
- Concrete examples
- Transparent pricing

## Don't
- Robot or brain stock images
- Dark sci-fi clichés
- Buzzword walls

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Figtree:400,600,800.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
