# Sport Energy

Match-day energy: black and fiery red, condensed uppercase headlines, diagonal cuts. Mood: Powerful, fast, passionate.

## Principles
- Big uppercase type and action photography.
- Diagonal cuts and stripes create motion.
- Next match, results and tickets are always visible.

## Layout
Full-bleed action hero with a slanted bottom edge, next-match countdown card, results table, news grid, squad cards, sponsors strip.

## Typography
Condensed uppercase headlines 48–96px; body Inter 16px/1.6; scores in big tabular numbers.

## Color
Black base, red primary, yellow accent for highlights (live, win), dark surfaces for tables.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 4px radius, red fill, uppercase condensed label.
- **Cards:** Dark surface, image with red gradient overlay, uppercase title.
- **Navigation:** Black bar, crest left, tickets button right.
- **Forms:** Dark inputs with red focus.

## Imagery & icons
High-energy action shots, crests, bold pictograms.

## Motion
Fast 150ms slides; diagonal wipes.

## Do
- Countdown to the next match
- Tables and fixtures
- Diagonal shapes

## Don't
- Pastel colours
- Thin serif type
- Static, quiet layouts

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Oswald:500,700, Inter:400,600.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
