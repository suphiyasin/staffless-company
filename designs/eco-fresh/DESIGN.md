# Eco Fresh

Clean and natural: leafy greens, airy white space, organic curves. Mood: Fresh, honest, optimistic.

## Principles
- Nature photography and real impact numbers.
- Airy layouts; nothing cramped.
- Organic curves soften the grid.

## Layout
Hero with a landscape photo and a curved bottom edge, impact stats, mission sections, donate/shop CTA band.

## Typography
Geometric sans. Headlines 40–64px. Body 17px/1.7.

## Color
Forest green primary, honey accent, pale green surfaces.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 12px radius, green fill, honey for secondary highlights.
- **Cards:** White, 24px radius, leaf-tinted icon.
- **Navigation:** Clean white bar.
- **Forms:** Rounded inputs, green focus.

## Imagery & icons
Natural landscapes, people outdoors, leaf line icons.

## Motion
Gentle fades.

## Do
- Impact numbers
- Curved section edges
- Natural photos

## Don't
- Neon colours
- Heavy shadows
- Greenwashing claims without data

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Manrope:400,600,800.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
