# Playful Kids

Primary colours and chunky shapes: friendly, big, impossible to miss. Mood: Joyful, energetic, safe.

## Principles
- Big touch targets and big text.
- Each section gets its own bright colour.
- Simple words, short sentences.

## Layout
Colourful stacked sections with wavy dividers, large illustrated cards, one action per section.

## Typography
Rounded display font. Headlines 44–72px. Body 19px/1.6.

## Color
Orange primary, blue accent, plus sunny yellow (#ffbe0b) and grass green (#06d6a0) for sections.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Chunky rounded buttons with a solid bottom shadow that presses down.
- **Cards:** Thick outline, big radius, illustration on top.
- **Navigation:** Large icon-and-label links.
- **Forms:** Big rounded fields, friendly validation.

## Imagery & icons
Cheerful illustrations, characters, big icons.

## Motion
Bouncy 250ms springs.

## Do
- Bright section colours
- Large buttons
- Illustrations

## Don't
- Small text
- Muted greys
- Dense paragraphs

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Baloo 2:500,700,800.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
