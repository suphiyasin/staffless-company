# Yoga Calm

A quiet studio: sand, sage and clay, slow rhythm and lots of breathing room. Mood: Grounded, peaceful, gentle.

## Principles
- Slow down the page: generous spacing, short paragraphs, one idea per section.
- Natural palette only: sand, sage, clay.
- Booking a class is calm but obvious.

## Layout
Soft hero with a photo in an arch-shaped frame and a short invitation. Class types in a 3-column grid, a weekly schedule, teacher portraits in arches, testimonials, intro offer band.

## Typography
Marcellus headlines 36–60px. Mulish body 17px/1.8. Small labels in letter-spaced uppercase.

## Color
Sand base, sage green primary, clay accent for small highlights, deep green-grey text.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Pill shape, sage fill or sage outline.
- **Cards:** Sand surface, 24px radius, arch-cropped image, class name and length.
- **Navigation:** Light, centred logo, Book button as a pill.
- **Forms:** Soft rounded inputs on the surface colour.

## Imagery & icons
Natural light, plants, linen, bodies in calm poses; thin organic line icons.

## Motion
Slow 400ms fades; nothing that pops.

## Do
- Arch-shaped image frames
- Lots of space
- Intro offer

## Don't
- Bright saturated colours
- Hard shadows
- Crowded schedules

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Marcellus:400, Mulish:400,600.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
