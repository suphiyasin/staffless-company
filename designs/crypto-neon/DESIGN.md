# Crypto Neon

Neon terminal: deep space navy, electric cyan and magenta glows, techno headlines. Mood: Futuristic, electric, bold.

## Principles
- Glow is the signature: cyan and magenta light on deep navy.
- Live data everywhere: prices, volume, block height.
- Clear, honest risk and fee information despite the hype look.

## Layout
Hero with a glowing headline, a live price ticker strip, token stats cards, roadmap timeline, how-it-works steps, FAQ, community links.

## Typography
Orbitron uppercase headlines 40–80px. Exo 2 body 16px/1.6. Numbers in tabular figures.

## Color
Space navy base, cyan primary with glow, magenta accent for highlights; green/red only for price changes.

## Components
- **Buttons:** 6px radius, cyan fill with navy text and a cyan glow; secondary cyan outline.
- **Cards:** Navy surface, 1px cyan-tinted border, glow on hover.
- **Navigation:** Dark, Connect wallet button in cyan.
- **Forms:** Dark inputs with cyan focus glow.

## Imagery & icons
Abstract 3D shapes, grids and glows; line icons with glow.

## Motion
Ticker scrolls; glows pulse slowly (disabled for reduced motion).

## Do
- Live ticker
- Roadmap timeline
- Risk disclaimers

## Don't
- Light backgrounds
- Serif fonts
- Unreadable glow on body text

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Orbitron:600,800, Exo 2:400,600.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
