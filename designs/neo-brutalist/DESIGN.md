# Neo-Brutalist

Loud and honest: thick black borders, hard offset shadows, flat candy colours. Mood: Bold, funny, unpolished on purpose.

## Principles
- Everything has a 3px black border and a hard shadow.
- Flat colour blocks; no gradients, no blur.
- Big type, short sentences, a little humour.

## Layout
Stacked blocks in alternating colours, slightly rotated stickers and badges, chunky grid of cards.

## Typography
Headlines 48–88px bold. Body 18px. Highlight words with a coloured background.

## Color
Off-white base, yellow primary, pink accent, plus one of mint (#8cf2c8) or sky (#9ad8ff) for blocks.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Yellow fill, black border, hard shadow that collapses on press.
- **Cards:** White, black border, hard shadow, coloured header strip.
- **Navigation:** Bordered bar with blocky links.
- **Forms:** Thick-bordered inputs, hard shadow on focus.

## Imagery & icons
Flat illustrations, stickers, emoji-style icons.

## Motion
Snappy 100ms translate on hover/press.

## Do
- Hard shadows
- Colour blocks
- Playful copy

## Don't
- Soft shadows
- Thin grey borders
- Corporate tone

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Space Grotesk:500,700.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
