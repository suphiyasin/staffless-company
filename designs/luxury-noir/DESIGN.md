# Luxury Noir

Quiet luxury: black and warm gold, high-contrast serif, wide letter spacing. Mood: Exclusive, refined, restrained.

## Principles
- Less is more: few elements, lots of black space.
- Gold is jewellery: thin lines and small details only.
- Photography is the product; let it breathe edge to edge.

## Layout
Full-screen imagery, centred small uppercase headings, slow vertical rhythm, two-column editorial blocks.

## Typography
Serif headlines uppercase with wide tracking, 32–64px. Body Montserrat 15px/1.8, light.

## Color
Black, warm ivory text, gold for lines, small labels and the primary button outline.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Thin gold outline, uppercase, wide tracking, no radius.
- **Cards:** Image-led, caption below in small caps.
- **Navigation:** Centred logo, minimal links, lots of space.
- **Forms:** Underlined inputs, gold focus line.

## Imagery & icons
Dark, moody, high-end photography.

## Motion
Slow 400ms fades and image reveals.

## Do
- Wide letter spacing
- Full-bleed imagery
- Thin gold rules

## Don't
- Bright colours
- Busy layouts
- Discount badges

## Using this design
- Link `design/tokens.css` before your own stylesheet and use its variables (`var(--color-primary)`, `var(--font-heading)`, `var(--radius)`…) instead of hard-coded values.
- Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Cormorant Garamond:500,700, Montserrat:400,500.
- Adapt it to the project (content, sections, imagery); keep the principles and the tokens.
